NASA Public Domain / Media Usage audio samples
==============================================

All audio files in this folder are downloaded from NASA's Historical Sounds
collection for educational / visualization use.

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/historical-sounds/
Media guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Included (filenames → original NASA titles):
- sts131_launch.mp3      Discovery STS-131: Sound of Launch
- sts135_countdown.mp3   STS-135: Countdown to Launch
- sts135_commentary.mp3  STS-135: Launch Commentary
- sts26_liftoff.mp3      Discovery – STS-26 Liftoff
- sts41d_liftoff.mp3     Discovery – STS-41D Liftoff
- apollo11_liftoff.mp3   Apollo 11: We Have a Lift-Off
- throttle_up.mp3        Discovery – Go at throttle up
- roger_roll.mp3         Discovery – Roger roll
- meco.mp3               Discovery – MECO
- orbit_nice.mp3         Discovery – Nice to be in orbit
- atlas_v_launch.mp3     Atlas V: Launch
- sls_testfire.mp3       SLS Test Fire
- quindar1.mp3 / quindar2.mp3  Quindar tones
- saturn_radio.mp3       Cassini: Saturn Radio Emissions
- chorus.mp3             Chorus Radio Waves within Earth's Atmosphere (EMFISIS / Univ. of Iowa)

Not affiliated with NASA or SpaceX. For immersive Starship visualization only.
