                         2k source upscaled to 4K; CC BY 4.0

  earth_day_4k.jpg     — SSS 2k day map original
  earth_night_hi.jpg   — NASA Black Marble 2016 city lights (4K)

                         https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73934

                         examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg
  earth_clouds_hi.jpg  — 4K fair-weather clouds (turban/webgl-earth)
  earth_clouds_4k.jpg  — Solar System Scope 2k clouds (CC BY 4.0)
  earth_normal.jpg     — three.js r172 examples/textures/planets
  earth_spec.jpg       — three.js r172 examples/textures/planets
  earth_lights.png     — three.js r172 examples/textures/planets
  earth_clouds.jpg     — threex.planets earthcloudmap

Note: Planetary "atmosphere glow" (blue limb) is not a UV texture — it is
rendered with a Fresnel / Rayleigh limb shader (src/scene/earthShaders.js).
Real satellite data for atmosphere is the cloud opacity map above.

NASA Visible Earth imagery is public domain (US government work).
Solar System Scope textures: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

webgl-earth textures: https://github.com/turban/webgl-earth
  (imagery derived from NASA public-domain Blue Marble / elevation data)
